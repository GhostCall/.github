
# GhostCall

## What is GhostCall?

GhostCall is a browser-based voice calling platform that lets people start temporary conversations through a simple shareable link.

No account creation, phone number exchange, or app installation required.

Create a call link, share it with someone, and start talking.

## Features

- **One-to-one calls** — Have private voice conversations.
- **Group calls** — Bring up to four participants into one call.
- **Link-based calling** — Invite people using a simple shareable link.
- **No signup required** — Start or join calls without creating an account.
- **Browser-based** — Works on supported mobile and desktop browsers.
- **Temporary rooms** — Call rooms are not intended to become permanent communication spaces.

## Who is GhostCall for?

- People buying or selling online.
- People meeting someone new.
- Teams having quick discussions.
- Groups that need a temporary voice conversation.

## Our vision

We believe not every conversation needs to become a permanent connection.

GhostCall is being built around a simple idea: people should have more choice over how they communicate.
